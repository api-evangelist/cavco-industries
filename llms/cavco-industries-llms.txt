# Cavco Industries

> Cavco Industries, Inc. (Nasdaq: CVCO), founded in Phoenix, Arizona in 1965, designs and builds
> factory-built housing — manufactured homes, modular homes, park model RVs and vacation cabins — at
> more than twenty plants across the United States, sold through independent and company-owned
> retailers under the Cavco family of brands (including Fleetwood Homes and Palm Harbor Homes), with
> financing through CountryPlace Mortgage and homeowner insurance through Standard Casualty.

## Machine-readable API surface

**Cavco Industries publishes no API.** As of 2026-09-19 the company operates no developer program,
no developer portal, no SDK, no GitHub organization, and no machine-readable contract of any kind.
This was established by probe, not by assumption:

- The hosts `developer.cavco.com` and `api.cavco.com`, which an earlier profile of this company had
  recorded as its documentation and API base, do not exist in DNS (NXDOMAIN) and have been removed
  from the record.
- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, MCP server, A2A agent card or `llms.txt` was found on
  `cavco.com`, `www.cavco.com`, `cavcohomes.com`, `www.cavcohomes.com`, `investor.cavco.com`,
  `careers.cavco.com` or `cavconnect.cavco.com`. Every `/.well-known/` discovery path on every host
  404d, or answered with an SPA shell or captcha interstitial (see
  `well-known/cavco-industries-well-known.yml` for the full probe record).
- No package named for the company exists on npm, PyPI, RubyGems or NuGet.
- The consumer brand site is a Bloomreach (brXM) React single-page app. Its `/resourceapi/*` endpoint
  serves the stock Bloomreach Pages Delivery API JSON that the SPA renders from. That is CMS vendor
  infrastructure, not a product API Cavco designed, documented or supports, and is deliberately NOT
  catalogued as a Cavco API.
- The one integration property Cavco runs is **CavConnect** (`https://cavconnect.cavco.com/`), an
  Angular retailer portal for its dealer network. It is login-gated, publishes no documentation, and
  its backend host (`cavconnectdata.cavco.com`, named in the portal's public bundle) does not resolve
  from the public internet. It is a dealer application, not a developer surface.

If you are an agent looking for a callable Cavco Industries API, there is not one to call. Retailer,
lender and supplier relationships are contracted privately.
